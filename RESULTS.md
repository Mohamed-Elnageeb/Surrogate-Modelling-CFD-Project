## 5. Prediction accuracy depends on which experiment you choose

NACA0012 at Re=6e6, scored against NASA-hosted experimental data.

| Comparison | n | mean abs. error | bias |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drag vs Ladson 1988 (**tripped**) | 35 | **39.6%** | -39.6% |
| Drag vs Abbott & von Doenhoff (**free transition**) | 12 | **11.9%** | -11.9% |
| Lift vs Ladson 1988 | 24 | 4.1% | +3.4% |

The same model, on the same airfoil at the same Reynolds number, appears **3.3x** more accurate against one experiment than the other. NeuralFoil inherits XFOIL's transition model, so it predicts a laminar run over the forward chord and cannot match a tripped experiment; the bias is negative by construction.

Drag error is **9.7x** the lift error, so a benchmark reporting lift or field-MSE would not reveal this. Drag is the discriminating quantity.
